Age in Months Calculator

Enter a date of birth to see an age in total months, weeks and days. Useful for babies, toddlers and growth checks.

How it works

The tool counts the full calendar months between the date of birth and today (or the date you choose), then shows the leftover days. Total weeks and days are also listed, which parents often need for vaccination schedules and baby milestone charts.

This is a general calculator. For medical or growth decisions, follow your doctor's guidance.

Example

A child born on 15 January 2024 is 27 months and 5 days old on 20 April 2026. That is 2 years and 3 months, or 118 weeks. The tool shows all of these at once so you do not need to count by hand.

Quick reference

6 months is half a year, 12 months is 1 year, 18 months is 1 year and 6 months, 24 months is 2 years, 30 months is 2.5 years, and 36 months is 3 years. To turn years into months, multiply by 12 and add the extra months.

Why age in months matters

Vaccination schedules, baby growth charts and developmental checklists for young children are usually written in months rather than years. Use this calculator to find the right row quickly, and follow your paediatrician's advice for any medical decision. For an adult age in years, months and days, try the age calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate age in months?

Multiply the full years by 12 and add the extra months. For example, 2 years and 3 months is 27 months.

Is this useful for babies?

Yes. Doctors and growth charts often use age in months for children under 2 or 3 years.

Can I check age on a past date?

Yes. Fill in the Age on field with any date after the date of birth.

How many months is 2.5 years?

2.5 years is 30 months.

How do I calculate the months between two dates?

Count the full calendar months from the first date to the second. This tool does it for you and also shows the extra days.
