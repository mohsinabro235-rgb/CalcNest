Zakat Calculator

Add your savings, gold, silver, business stock and debts to estimate the zakat you owe. You set the current gold or silver price yourself.

How it works

Zakat is generally calculated as 2.5% of your net zakatable wealth when that wealth stays above the nisab for one full lunar year. This tool adds your cash, gold, silver, business stock and money owed to you, subtracts your debts, and compares the total with the nisab you choose.

The nisab is worth 52.5 tola of silver or 7.5 tola of gold, so you enter today's price per tola. Scholars and schools of thought differ on which basis to use and on some assets, so this is an estimate only. Please confirm your final amount with a qualified scholar.

Worked example

Suppose a person has Rs. 800,000 in savings, gold worth Rs. 300,000 and owes Rs. 100,000 in short-term debts. Net zakatable wealth is 800,000 + 300,000 - 100,000 = Rs. 1,000,000. For illustration only, assume silver costs Rs. 3,000 a tola, so the silver nisab is 52.5 x 3,000 = Rs. 157,500. The wealth is above the nisab, so zakat is 2.5% of Rs. 1,000,000, which is Rs. 25,000. This price is just an example, so enter today's real price in the calculator.

What is usually counted

  • Cash in hand and in bank accounts.
  • Gold and silver, and the value of trade goods or business stock.
  • Money owed to you that you expect to receive.
  • Investments held for trade, depending on the type.

What is usually not counted

Most scholars do not count the house you live in or a car and household items that you use personally. Opinions differ on some assets, such as jewellery worn regularly, so ask a qualified scholar about your own case.

Timing

Zakat becomes due when your wealth stays above the nisab for a full lunar year. Many people pick one fixed date each year, for example in Ramadan, and calculate everything they own on that day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the nisab for zakat?

It is the minimum wealth above which zakat becomes due. It is commonly measured as 52.5 tola of silver or 7.5 tola of gold.

What percentage of zakat do I pay?

The usual rate on cash, gold, silver and trade goods is 2.5% of the zakatable wealth you have held for a lunar year.

Should I subtract my debts?

Many scholars allow deducting debts that are due. Because opinions differ, ask a scholar about your own case.

Do I pay zakat on my house?

Most scholars say the home you live in is not zakatable, but a property held for sale or trade is. Ask a scholar about your case.

Which nisab should I use, silver or gold?

Opinions differ, and the silver nisab is lower, so more people are liable under it. Please ask a scholar which basis you should follow.
