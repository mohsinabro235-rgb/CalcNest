Electricity Bill Calculator Pakistan

Enter your units, consumer type and the adjustments shown on your bill to estimate your monthly electricity bill for a household connection.

How it works

The estimate multiplies your units by the base residential rate for your slab, then adds the fuel and quarterly adjustments, any fixed charges and GST. This tool charges all of your units at the rate of the slab your total falls in, which is why a few extra units near 100, 200 or 300 can raise the bill noticeably.

Base rates here follow the NEPRA residential tariff that the government kept unchanged for 2026. Real bills also include things like electricity duty, TV fee, surcharges and withholding tax, and each distribution company (LESCO, IESCO, K-Electric and others) can differ. Fuel adjustments change every month, so copy the per-unit figure from your latest bill. Treat the result as an estimate and rely on your printed bill for the exact amount.

Why the bill jumps at 200 and 300 units

This calculator charges all units at the rate of the slab your total falls in. A protected household using 200 units pays about Rs. 2,602 in energy charges. At 201 units it becomes unprotected and the same household pays about Rs. 6,653, which is more than double, even though it used a single extra unit. The same kind of jump happens at the other slab boundaries.

How to estimate your real bill

  1. Enter your units and consumer type.
  2. Look at your latest bill for the fuel and quarterly adjustment per unit and any fixed charge, and type them in.
  3. Keep the GST box ticked to add 18% to the total.
  4. Compare the result with your printed bill. Small differences usually come from duty, surcharges, TV fee and withholding tax.

Ways to keep the bill down

  • Check your meter near the end of the month and try to stay below the next slab boundary.
  • Use air conditioners and heaters carefully, since they use the most units.
  • Switch to LED lights and efficient appliances.

Frequently asked questions

What is a protected consumer?

A protected consumer is a household that uses 200 units or less each month. They pay lower subsidized rates, and they lose protected status if consumption goes above that.

Why is my real bill higher than this estimate?

Bills include extra items such as electricity duty, surcharges, TV fee and taxes, and adjustments change each month. Add the figures printed on your own bill for a closer estimate.

How can I lower my bill?

Staying under 200 units, especially if you are protected, makes the biggest difference. Efficient appliances and using less air conditioning also cut units.

Why does my bill jump when I cross 200 units?

Because you lose protected status and your units are charged at the higher unprotected rates, so even one extra unit can change the bill a lot.

Does this match my DISCO bill exactly?

No. It is an estimate. Your distribution company and the charges printed on your bill decide the final amount.
