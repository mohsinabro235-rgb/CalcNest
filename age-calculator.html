Age Calculator: Umar Saal, Mahine aur Din mein

Date of birth dalein aur apni exact umar, agli birthday tak baqi din aur total din dekhein.

Ye kaise kaam karta hai

Date of birth ko aaj ki date (ya aap ki chuni hui date) se minus kiya jata hai. Calculator leap years aur mahinon ke alag dinon ka khayal rakhta hai.

Aksar pooche gaye sawal

Kya ye calculator CNIC ya form ke liye sahi hai?

Ye aam maloomat ke liye hai. Sarkari form mein hamesha apne documents wali date use karein.

Kya main kisi purani date tak umar nikal sakta hoon?

Ji haan, 'Is date tak' mein koi bhi date chunein.
