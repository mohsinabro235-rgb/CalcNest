Age Calculator

Enter your date of birth to see your exact age, total days lived and the days left until your next birthday.

How it works

The calculator subtracts your date of birth from today's date, or from any date you choose in the optional field. It borrows days and months the way a calendar does, so leap years and months of different lengths are handled correctly.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator handle leap years?

Yes. It uses real calendar dates, so February 29 and months with 28, 30 or 31 days are counted correctly.

Can I find my age on a specific date?

Yes. Fill in the "Age on" field with any date after your birth date, such as a past exam date or a future deadline.

Is my date of birth stored anywhere?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.
