Unit Converter

Convert between common units, including local ones such as tola, marla and kanal, with instant results as you type.

How it works

Every unit is converted through a common base unit (meter, kilogram, square meter or liter), so any pair of units in a category works. Temperature uses its own formulas, for example Fahrenheit = Celsius x 9/5 + 32. Marla is taken as 272.25 square feet and kanal as 20 marla.

Frequently asked questions

How many square feet are in a marla?

Using the standard value, one marla is 272.25 square feet, and one kanal is 20 marla (5,445 square feet). Local definitions can vary slightly by region.

How accurate are the conversions?

They use exact international definitions for the common units, so results are accurate enough for everyday and most professional use.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Choose Temperature, set From to Celsius and To to Fahrenheit, then type the value. The formula is C x 9/5 + 32.
