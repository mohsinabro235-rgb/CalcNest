BMI Calculator

Enter your weight in kilograms and height in centimeters to see your BMI, your WHO category and a healthy weight range for your height.

How it works

BMI is your weight in kilograms divided by your height in meters squared. The World Health Organization classifies a BMI below 18.5 as underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 as normal weight, 25 to 29.9 as overweight and 30 or above as obese.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI always accurate?

No. BMI does not separate muscle from fat, so athletes and very muscular people can get a high BMI without excess body fat. It is a screening tool, not a diagnosis.

What is a healthy BMI?

For most adults, a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered the normal range.

Should I use this for medical decisions?

No. This calculator gives a general estimate only. Talk to a doctor about your health and weight goals.
