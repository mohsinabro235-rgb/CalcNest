BMI Calculator: Apna Body Mass Index Maloom Karein

Wazan (kg) aur qad (cm) dalein aur WHO ke mutabiq apna BMI aur category dekhein.

Ye kaise kaam karta hai

BMI = wazan (kg) / qad (meter) ka square. WHO ke mutabiq 18.5 se kam kam wazan, 18.5 se 24.9 normal, 25 se 29.9 zyada wazan, aur 30 ya zyada motapa hai.

Aksar pooche gaye sawal

Kya BMI hamesha durust hota hai?

Nahi. BMI muscle aur charbi mein farq nahi karta, is liye khilariyon ya bachon ke liye ye poori tasveer nahi dikhata.

Sehat ke faislon ke liye kya karein?

Ye sirf andaza hai. Sehat ke baare mein doctor se mashwara karein.
