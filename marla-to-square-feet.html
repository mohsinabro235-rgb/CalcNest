Marla to Square Feet

Convert marla to square feet instantly, and see the same area in kanal, square yards, square meters and acres.

How it works

This converter uses the standard housing-scheme value of 1 marla = 272.25 square feet (30.25 square yards, about 25.29 square meters), and 1 kanal = 20 marla = 5,445 square feet. Type a value, pick the unit you are converting from, and every other area unit updates instantly.

Local definitions can differ. Some areas and housing societies use a slightly different marla, so always confirm the measurement written in your property documents before you buy or sell.

Common plot sizes in square feet

Using 1 marla = 272.25 square feet: a 3 marla plot is 816.75 sq ft, a 5 marla plot is 1,361.25 sq ft, a 7 marla plot is 1,905.75 sq ft, and a 10 marla plot is 2,722.5 sq ft. Property dealers often round these numbers, so always ask for the exact covered and total area.

How to convert square feet to marla

Divide the area in square feet by 272.25. For example, a plot that measures 30 ft x 45 ft has 1,350 square feet. Divide 1,350 by 272.25 and you get about 4.96 marla, which is usually sold as a 5 marla plot. For a larger plot of 50 ft x 90 ft, the area is 4,500 square feet, which is about 16.5 marla or 0.83 kanal.

Before you buy or sell land

  • Check the area written in the registry, fard or society file, not only what the agent says.
  • Ask which marla size the housing scheme uses, since a few local systems use a different value.
  • Compare the price per square foot, not the price per marla, when you are looking at several plots.

Frequently asked questions

How many square feet are in 1 marla?

One marla is 272.25 square feet under the standard measurement used by most housing schemes in Pakistan.

How many marla are in a kanal?

One kanal equals 20 marla, which is 5,445 square feet.

Is the marla the same everywhere?

Not always. Some regions and societies use a slightly different size, so check your property papers before making a deal.

How do I convert square feet to marla?

Divide the square feet by 272.25. A 1,361.25 sq ft plot, for example, is 5 marla.

How many square yards are in a marla?

One marla is 30.25 square yards.

What is the size of a 10 marla plot?

A 10 marla plot is 2,722.5 square feet, about 302.5 square yards or 253 square meters.
