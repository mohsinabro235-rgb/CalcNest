Kanal to Marla

Convert kanal to marla and see the equivalent area in square feet, square yards, square meters and acres.

How it works

The calculator uses 1 kanal = 20 marla, with 1 marla equal to 272.25 square feet. That makes 1 kanal 5,445 square feet, 605 square yards or about 505.86 square meters.

Enter the number of kanal, or pick another unit such as marla, square feet or acres, and you will see all the other values at once. Check your local land record, since the size of a marla can vary in some regions.

How to convert kanal to marla

Multiply the number of kanal by 20. So 2 kanal is 40 marla, and half a kanal is 10 marla. To go the other way, divide marla by 20: a 12 marla plot is 0.6 kanal, and a 15 marla plot is 0.75 kanal.

Kanal in other units

One kanal is 5,445 square feet, 605 square yards and about 505.86 square meters. Because an acre is 43,560 square feet, exactly 8 kanal make one acre. A farmhouse plot of 4 kanal is therefore half an acre, or 80 marla.

Tips for land deals

  • Write down the area in square feet in the agreement so there is no confusion over the marla size.
  • Ask the seller for the measurement from the official record.
  • Use the converter above to double-check any figure an agent gives you.
