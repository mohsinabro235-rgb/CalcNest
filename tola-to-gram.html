Tola to Gram

Convert gold and silver weights between tola, masha, ratti, grams, kilograms and troy ounces.

How it works

The tool uses the traditional South Asian jeweller's units: 1 tola = 11.6638 grams, 1 tola = 12 masha, and 1 masha = 8 ratti. Choose the unit you have, and the weight in every other unit appears at once.

Some shops round a tola to 11.66 grams, so the last digits can differ slightly from a jeweller's scale.

Quick examples

5 tola of gold weighs about 58.32 grams, and 10 tola weighs about 116.64 grams. A single masha is 0.972 grams and a ratti is 0.1215 grams. In the other direction, 100 grams is about 8.57 tola, and 1 troy ounce (31.1035 grams) is about 2.667 tola.

Using the converter with gold prices

International gold is quoted per troy ounce, while local jewellers quote per tola. To get a rough price per tola from an ounce price, divide the ounce price by 2.667. Local premiums, making charges and purity (karat) change the final price, so this only gives a starting point.

Why shops may differ slightly

Some jewellers round a tola to 11.66 grams and others use 11.664 grams. The difference is tiny on one tola but grows on large purchases, so check the weight on a digital scale and ask which value the shop uses.

Frequently asked questions

How many grams are in 1 tola?

One tola is about 11.66 grams (11.6638 g).

How many masha are in a tola?

One tola is 12 masha, and each masha is 8 ratti.

Is this the same for gold and silver?

Yes. A tola is a unit of weight, so it is the same for any metal. Only the price per tola differs.

How many tola are in 100 grams?

100 grams is about 8.57 tola.

How many tola are in a troy ounce?

One troy ounce is about 2.667 tola.

How many ratti are in a tola?

One tola is 96 ratti, because it has 12 masha and each masha has 8 ratti.
