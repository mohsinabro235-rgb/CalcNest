How Salary Tax Is Calculated in Pakistan (2026-27)

A simple explanation of Pakistan's salaried income tax slabs for tax year 2027, with step-by-step examples for different monthly salaries.

Salary tax in Pakistan is progressive. That means different parts of your income are taxed at different rates, and a higher salary does not push your whole income into a higher rate. This guide explains how the calculation works under the Finance Act 2026, which applies from 1 July 2026 (tax year 2027).

What changed for tax year 2027

The first Rs. 600,000 of annual income is still tax free, and the first two slabs are the same as before. The government cut rates in four middle and upper salary brackets, raised the point where the 35% rate begins to Rs. 7 million, and removed the 9% surcharge for salaried individuals.

How to calculate your tax

  1. Multiply your monthly salary by 12 to get your annual taxable salary.
  2. Find the slab that contains this figure.
  3. Add the fixed tax for that slab to the percentage of the amount above the slab's lower limit.
  4. Divide the result by 12 to see the monthly deduction.

Example: Rs. 100,000 a month

Annual salary is Rs. 1,200,000. The first Rs. 600,000 is free and the next Rs. 600,000 is taxed at 1%, which is Rs. 6,000 a year or Rs. 500 a month.

Example: Rs. 200,000 a month

Annual salary is Rs. 2,400,000. This is in the 20% slab. Tax = 116,000 + 20% of 200,000 = Rs. 156,000 a year, or Rs. 13,000 a month.

What these slabs do not cover

These rates are for salaried individuals, meaning people whose salary is more than 75% of their taxable income. Business owners, freelancers and people with large non-salary income are taxed differently. Deductions and tax credits, such as approved donations or pension contributions, can lower the final figure, so confirm your exact tax with FBR or a tax advisor.
