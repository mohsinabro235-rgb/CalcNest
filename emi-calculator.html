EMI Calculator

Enter the loan amount, yearly interest rate and tenure to see your monthly EMI, total interest and total repayment.

How it works

EMI stands for equated monthly installment. The formula is EMI = P x r x (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate divided by 12 and by 100) and n is the number of monthly installments.

Frequently asked questions

Will my bank's EMI match this result?

It is usually very close, but processing fees, insurance and the bank's own rounding can change the final figure.

How can I reduce my EMI?

Choose a longer tenure, make a larger down payment or find a lower interest rate. A longer tenure lowers the EMI but increases the total interest you pay.

Can I use this for car, home or personal loans?

Yes. The formula is the same for any loan with fixed monthly installments and a fixed interest rate.
