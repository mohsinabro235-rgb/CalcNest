EMI Calculator: Loan ki Mahana Qist Maloom Karein

Loan ki raqam, sood ki sala sharah aur muddat dalein. Mahana EMI, kul sood aur kul adaigi foran dekhein.

Ye kaise kaam karta hai

Formula: EMI = P x r x (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1). Yahan P loan ki raqam, r mahana sood (sala sood / 12 / 100) aur n qiston ki tadad hai.

Aksar pooche gaye sawal

Kya bank ki EMI bilkul yehi hogi?

Aksar qareeb hoti hai, lekin processing fee, insurance aur bank ka apna hisab farq la sakta hai.

Kya Islamic financing ke liye bhi chalega?

Markup/profit rate ko sood ki jagah dal kar andaza lagaya ja sakta hai. Asal shartein apne bank se confirm karein.
