Word Counter

Paste or type your text to instantly count words, characters, sentences and paragraphs, and see how long it takes to read.

How it works

Words are counted as groups of characters separated by spaces or line breaks. Sentences are counted by ending punctuation, and paragraphs by blank lines. Reading time assumes an average speed of 200 words per minute.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text saved or uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and your text is never sent to a server.

Does it work for Urdu and other languages?

Yes. Words are split by spaces, so it works for any language that separates words with spaces.

How is reading time calculated?

It divides the word count by 200, the average adult silent reading speed, and rounds up to the next minute.
