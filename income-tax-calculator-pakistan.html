Income Tax Calculator Pakistan

Enter your monthly or annual salary to see your income tax, monthly deduction and take-home salary under the Finance Act 2026 slabs.

How it works

This calculator applies the salaried-individual slabs for tax year 2027 (income earned from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027) as set out in the Finance Act 2026, which was enacted on 26 June 2026. The first Rs. 600,000 of annual income is tax free, and the extra 9% surcharge on high salaries no longer applies.

The slabs below are for individuals whose salary is more than 75% of their taxable income. Business owners, freelancers and people with other income are taxed under different rules. The tool does not include deductions, tax credits, allowances or other income, so your actual tax can be lower. Please check the final figure with FBR or a tax advisor.

Step by step: Rs. 200,000 a month

  1. Convert to an annual figure: 200,000 x 12 = Rs. 2,400,000.
  2. Find the slab. Rs. 2,400,000 falls in the Rs. 2,200,001 to Rs. 3,200,000 slab.
  3. Take the fixed tax for that slab: Rs. 116,000.
  4. Add 20% of the amount above Rs. 2,200,000. That is 20% of Rs. 200,000 = Rs. 40,000.
  5. Annual tax = 116,000 + 40,000 = Rs. 156,000, which is Rs. 13,000 a month.
  6. Take-home pay = 200,000 - 13,000 = Rs. 187,000 a month.

Step by step: Rs. 500,000 a month

Annual salary is Rs. 6,000,000, which sits in the Rs. 5,600,001 to Rs. 7,000,000 slab. Tax = 976,000 + 32% of (6,000,000 - 5,600,000) = 976,000 + 128,000 = Rs. 1,104,000 a year, or Rs. 92,000 a month. The effective rate is 18.4%, much lower than the 32% marginal rate, because only the income above each slab limit is taxed at the higher rate.

Marginal rate and effective rate

The rate in the table applies only to the part of your income inside that slab. So a raise does not make your whole salary taxable at a higher rate, only the extra amount. The effective rate is your total tax divided by your total income. You can read a full walkthrough in our guide to how salary tax is calculated in Pakistan.

Frequently asked questions

Is a salary of Rs. 50,000 a month taxable?

Annual income up to Rs. 600,000, which is Rs. 50,000 a month, is exempt under the tax year 2027 slabs.

When do these rates apply?

They apply to salary earned from 1 July 2026. Income earned before that date falls under the earlier tax year slabs.

Does this include deductions and credits?

No. It uses only the slab rates. Approved donations, pension contributions and other allowed deductions or credits can reduce your actual tax.

Who counts as a salaried individual?

The FBR treats a person as salaried when salary is more than 75% of their total taxable income. Other taxpayers are taxed under different slabs.

Is there a surcharge on high salaries now?

For salaried individuals, the 9% surcharge on income above Rs. 10 million has been removed from tax year 2027.

Do I still need to file a return if my employer deducts tax?

Yes. Employers deduct tax monthly, but salaried persons still file an annual return through the FBR IRIS portal.
