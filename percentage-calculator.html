Percentage Calculator: Percent, Izafa aur Kami Nikalein

Teen aam hisab ek jagah: kisi raqam ka percent, ek raqam dosre ka kitna percent, aur percent change.

Ye kaise kaam karta hai

Percent matlab 'sau mein se'. X ka Y% = X x Y / 100. X, Y ka kitna % = X / Y x 100. Percent change = (naya - purana) / purana x 100.

Aksar pooche gaye sawal

Discount kaise nikalein?

Pehli option mein asal qeemat X aur discount % Y dalein. Jawab discount ki raqam hai, qeemat mein se minus karein.

Negative jawab ka kya matlab?

Percent change mein negative matlab kami (decrease) hai.
