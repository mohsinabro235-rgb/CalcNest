Percentage Calculator

Solve the three most common percentage problems in one place: percent of a number, what percent one number is of another, and percentage change.

How it works

Percent means "out of one hundred". X% of Y equals X x Y / 100. To find what percent X is of Y, divide X by Y and multiply by 100. Percentage change equals (new value - old value) / old value x 100.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a discount?

Choose "What is X% of Y?", enter the discount percent as X and the original price as Y. The result is the amount saved. Subtract it from the price to get the final cost.

What does a negative percentage change mean?

A negative result means the value decreased. A positive result means it increased.

Why can't the starting value be zero?

Percentage change divides by the starting value, and dividing by zero is undefined.
