Marla vs Kanal: What Is the Difference?

Understand marla and kanal, how they relate, their size in square feet and yards, and how to convert between them with examples.

If you have ever looked at a plot advertisement in Pakistan, you have seen the words marla and kanal. They are the two most common units for residential land, and mixing them up can cost a lot of money. This guide explains what each one means and how to convert between them.

What is a marla?

A marla is a traditional unit of land area. In most housing schemes today it is taken as 272.25 square feet, which is the same as 30.25 square yards or about 25.29 square meters. Plots are usually sold as 3, 5, 7, 10 or 12 marla.

What is a kanal?

A kanal is a bigger unit used for larger plots and houses. One kanal equals 20 marla, or 5,445 square feet, which is 605 square yards or about 505.86 square meters. Eight kanal make one acre.

How to convert

  • Marla to square feet: multiply by 272.25.
  • Square feet to marla: divide by 272.25.
  • Kanal to marla: multiply by 20.
  • Marla to kanal: divide by 20.

An example

A plot of 50 ft by 90 ft has an area of 4,500 square feet. Divide by 272.25 and you get about 16.5 marla, or roughly 0.83 kanal. If an advertisement calls it a 1 kanal plot, you now know it is smaller than one full kanal.

Things to check before buying

The size of a marla is not the same in every area. Some local systems and societies use a different value. Always compare the area in square feet written in the official record, and compare prices per square foot rather than per marla.
